# The ModelQuad name and logo

The ModelQuad **code** is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE):
you may use, copy, change and redistribute it, including in commercial
products, as that licence allows.

The **name** "ModelQuad"™ and the ModelQuad logo are not covered by that
licence (Apache 2.0, section 6). They belong to Raz Ben Aharon and identify
this project. This page says how you may use them, so that people can always
tell the original project from a copy.

## You may, without asking

- Say what is true: "works with ModelQuad", "a plugin for ModelQuad",
  "based on ModelQuad", "I contributed to ModelQuad".
- Use the name and logo to talk about the project: articles, talks, videos,
  tutorials, reviews, lists of tools, links to this repository.
- Keep the name and logo in an unmodified copy of this repository or release,
  for example a mirror or a package that installs it as it is.

## You must not

- Release a changed version, a fork, or a product built on this code under
  the name "ModelQuad", a name that could be confused with it (for example
  "ModelQuad Pro" or "ModelQuad Plus"), or with its logo. Give your version a
  name of its own, and you may say it is "based on ModelQuad".
- Suggest that the ModelQuad project or its author made, endorses, supports
  or is responsible for your product or service when that is not the case.
- Use the name in a domain name, package name, company name or social media
  account in a way that could make people think it is the official project.

## Other projects' names

ModelQuad is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by
or sponsored by Anthropic or OpenAI. "Claude" and "Claude Code" are
trademarks of Anthropic. "OpenAI" and "Codex" are trademarks of OpenAI.
ModelQuad uses those names only to say which tools it works with.

## Questions

If you are not sure whether a use is fine, open an issue on the repository
and ask. A request in good faith will get an answer, not a lawyer's letter.
